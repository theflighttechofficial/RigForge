## [1.0.0] - 2026-11-18

First public release. Live at https://rigforge-livid.vercel.app.

### Added
- Hardware catalog with 91 CPUs and 78 GPUs, priced in ₹ INR.
- Price-to-performance matrix with Pareto frontier.
- Head-to-head comparison with 6-axis workload radar charts.
- Bottleneck lab for 1080p, 1440p, 4K and workstation loads.
- Rig builder with socket checks, PSU sizing at 80% load and 18% GST breakdown.
- AI Build Doctor (Gemini, server-side) with an offline rules-based fallback.
- 3D assembly studio (Three.js) with exploded view, airflow view and clearance checks.
- FPS estimates, running-cost calculator with state DISCOM tariffs, upgrade ROI.
- Local hardware scan of your own PC (Windows, macOS, Linux agents).
- Sitemap generation, Vercel deployment, automated screenshot script.
- MIT license, Dockerfile and one-click Vercel deploy button.
